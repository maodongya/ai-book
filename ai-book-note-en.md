# When a sentence stops you, someone on the right is already explaining it

The real loss in a good book is not that it is hard. It is that you put the question down halfway through.

You skip the unfamiliar word, leave the allusion for later, and settle for a rough translation. By the time you want to ask, that page has already turned. AIBook keeps the moment on the same desk: your text on the left, explanation and translation on the right. When you want to read in quiet, put the assistant away and turn the page.

It is a reading companion for macOS. It does not choose your next bestseller, and it does not move your shelf into the cloud. Your text. Your model. Your pace.

## Read on the left. Understand on the right.

Open a chapter as `.txt`, or keep a whole book in one `.aibook`. The left page can be edited, selected, or filled by dropping a file in. Circle a passage you cannot follow, press once, and the explanation is written on the right.

An explanation is not the sentence copied out again. Classical Chinese can be explained in modern speech. A long passage can be followed by another question. The conversation for that book is still there the next time you open it. For close reading, translate word by word, with a short note beside each word. To read straight through, translate by paragraph, and the paragraphs stay the paragraphs. With the parallel view open, the right page lights up wherever the left page scrolls. Change the source too much, and alignment tells you it has broken. One click lines it up again. To hold a translation still, lock it.

One language does not have to be enough. Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, German, French, Italian, Latin, and Greek can each be written, in the order you choose, for explanation and for translation. The right page shows one language at a time. Switching to a language already generated does not ask the model again. Save an `.aibook`, and the source, those translations, and those explanations live in the same file.

## When you want to hear it, let it read

When your eyes are tired, listen. Source, explanation, or translation: a passage, or the whole piece. Faster for a sweep. Slower when you want every syllable. The system voice works offline. For a softer online voice, download it to your Mac first.

When your hands should stay on the page, hold the microphone and say one sentence. Save. Translate this passage. Enter reading mode. Read the whole text aloud. Say it clearly, and it runs. If the words were not caught, it only shows what it heard. It does not press a button at random.

When a textbook leaves you half a classic, Supplement Source can complete the passage in a voice close to the original. Then you read it, and you save it. Use this only on text you have the right to use.

## When you want to sink into the book, it steps aside

While you study, the window looks like an open book: leather, paper grain, a spine, type size and typeface as you choose them. When you want only the text, enter reading mode. Two pages side by side. The spine stays still, and one sheet curls over. Arrow keys, a click, or a swipe. Past about a third of the page, it turns. Short of that, it falls back. This file remembers which spread you reached.

The interface can change to your language too. Menus, the manual, and the privacy policy follow. Eight languages, plus Follow System.

## Your book. Your key.

Explanation, translation, and spoken commands use the model you enter in settings. OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, services commonly used in China, or any compatible address. The key stays on your Mac. Before text is sent, the interface names who will receive it.

AIBook does not alter your computer, does not upgrade its own code, and does not keep a sign-in-free cloud account for you. Library folders, reading position, and conversations stay on this Mac.

## Open it now

If you have a chapter that will not read smoothly, a translation that will not line up, or a classical piece you want to hear through, AIBook is ready for that page.

Search **AIBook** on the Mac App Store. After you download it, open Book Settings, enter your model key, and drop that page in.

The source is on the left. On the right, someone begins to explain.
